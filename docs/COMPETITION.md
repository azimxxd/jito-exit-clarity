## Required participation steps

1. The user signs in to TokenGems with their own wallet and completes their profile. The assistant must not connect or sign for the user.
2. Review the chosen project's page, product or documentation and accurately describe what was inspected. No trade, deposit, purchase or token ownership is required.
3. Publish one useful contribution on the project's TokenGems page; a standalone post must tag the project. Obtain the user's separate approval of the final text before publishing.
4. Follow `@tokengems` on X and repost the contest announcement before the deadline. These are eligibility checks rather than scoring inputs.
5. Use the TokenGems profile URL as the main Earn submission link, and provide the contribution/project links and X handle in the questions.

One contribution is sufficient. The rules allow one Earn entry per person, containing up to three contributions about different projects, with the strongest contribution judged. The project being reviewed must not be one the entrant owns, works for or is paid to promote. We review **JitoSOL**, not our own frontend prototype.

The listing judges specific observations, evidence of exploration and usefulness. A public GitHub repository and live prototype are supporting artifacts, not stated entry requirements. Keep submitted contributions public through judging, and do not edit them after the deadline.
